# VSTplugins
Windows VST3 synth and effects plugins

## Benzene ring modulator
Ring modulator inspired by old test equipment
* Signal generator feeds one side of the ring with periodic waveforms and several types of noise
* Ring modulation based on classic diode ring circuit with selectable silicon or germanium diodes, modeling quality, and circuit variables
* LFO, pitch tracking, and envelope follower modulation of all parameters

## Cricket synth
Lo-fi digital synth ported from a hardware synth running on Microchip dsPIC microcontroller, plus modern goodies
* Two oscillators, plus Osc 2 -> Osc 1 FM modulation and logic mixing (AND/OR/XOR)
* Amp and modulation envelopes and LFO with multiple mod destinations
* Filter section with selectable MS20-style LP and HP filters or comb filter
* Digital wrapping distortion for rude gnarliness

## Dragonfly multi-FX
4 sequential FX blocks, each of which can be MS20 LP or HP, comb filter, or Chebyshev polynomial waveshaper (or bypassed)
* 2 LFOs and an envelope follower to modulate effect params

## Moth phaser + distortion
Rat-style distortion into phaser modeled after Schulte Compact Phasing 'A'

## Silkworm reclockable reverb
Reverb based on Midiverb II algorithm + clock control that alters the speed at which the entire algorithm runs
